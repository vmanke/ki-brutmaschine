# 01 Universe observables

Status: ready-for-agent
Type: task

Q33. Every committed generation logs one `state` event with six observables:
performance (search solved rate, best and median), diversity (distinct shapes and prompt
building blocks, Shannon entropy of their frequencies), lineage tree (depth, branching,
share of surviving lineages), honesty (planted fakes caught, false alarms, confirmed
collusion; zero until overseers exist), judge skill and trust (distribution), and cost
(calls, tokens, wall time is the binary's). Pure computation in `src/cascade/`; the event
is part of the hash-chained log and covered by the resume-equivalence tests.
